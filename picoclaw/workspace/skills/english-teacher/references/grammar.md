# Grammar Catalogue

Quick-reference for common ESL grammar topics. Pick the topic matching the learner's question, explain the rule in plain language, then use the examples.

## Beginner

### Present simple vs present continuous
- Present simple: habits, facts — "I work at a bank."
- Present continuous: actions happening now — "I am working right now."
- Common error: "I am work at a bank." → "I work at a bank."

### Past simple
- Completed actions in the past — "She visited London last year."
- Irregular verbs: go→went, see→saw, eat→ate.

### Articles (a/an/the)
- a/an = one of many, first mention — "I saw a dog."
- the = specific/known — "The dog was barking."
- No article for general plural — "I like dogs."

### Prepositions of time/place
- Time: at 5 o'clock, on Monday, in June, in 2024.
- Place: at the door, on the table, in the box.

### Countable vs uncountable
- many books, much water, a lot of money.
- Common error: "many water" → "much water" / "a lot of water".

## Intermediate

### Present perfect vs past simple
- Present perfect: experience / result now — "I have visited Paris." (not when)
- Past simple: specific past time — "I visited Paris in 2019."
- Signals: ever, never, already, yet, just, since, for.

### Conditionals
- Zero: general truths — "If you heat ice, it melts."
- First: real future — "If it rains, I will stay home."
- Second: hypothetical — "If I had money, I would travel."
- Third: unreal past — "If I had studied, I would have passed."

### Modals
- can (ability), could (past/polite), should (advice), must (obligation), might (possibility).
- Common error: "I must to go." → "I must go."

### Reported speech
- Tense backshift: "I am tired" → She said she was tired.
- Pronoun and time changes: today→that day, now→then.

### Gerunds vs infinitives
- enjoy + -ing, want + to + verb.
- Common error: "I enjoy to play football." → "I enjoy playing football."

### Comparatives & superlatives
- short: big→bigger→biggest; long: expensive→more expensive→the most expensive.
- Common error: "more bigger" → "bigger".

## Advanced

### Mixed conditionals
- Past condition, present result: "If I had taken that job, I would be rich now."

### Inversion
- "Never have I seen such a thing." / "Not only did she sing, she also danced."

### Subjunctive / wishes
- "I wish I were taller." / "It's important that he be on time."

### Phrasal verbs
- carry out (do), come up with (invent), put up with (tolerate), look forward to (anticipate).

## Common Error Quick List

| Error | Correction |
|---|---|
| I am agree | I agree |
| She don't like | She doesn't like |
| I have 25 years | I am 25 years old |
| He is more taller | He is taller |
| I am here since Monday | I have been here since Monday |
| Can you explain me? | Can you explain it to me? |
| I look forward to hear from you | I look forward to hearing from you |
| I born in 1995 | I was born in 1995 |
| One of my friend | One of my friends |
| The people is nice | The people are nice |
