# Lesson & Exercise Templates

Use these structures when the learner wants a full lesson or a written assignment. Adapt length to the learner's level and available time.

## 30-Minute Lesson Plan

1. **Warm-up (3 min)** — Conversation question related to the topic ("What did you do last weekend?").
2. **Presentation (7 min)** — Introduce the target language with 3–5 example sentences in context.
3. **Practice (10 min)** — Controlled exercises: fill-in-the-blank, transformation, or matching.
4. **Production (7 min)** — Free use: the learner writes/says 3–5 sentences about themselves using the target language.
5. **Wrap-up (3 min)** — Recap the rule, correct remaining errors, set a mini task for next time.

## Writing Feedback Rubric

For essays, emails, or reports:

1. **Overall impression** (1–2 sentences): what works well.
2. **Critical errors** — fix with ❌ → ✅ → why. Prioritize: verb tense > subject-verb agreement > word order > articles/prepositions > word choice.
3. **Style improvements** — suggest 1–2 upgrades (linking words, more specific vocabulary, conciseness).
4. **Model rewrite** — for short texts (≤150 words), provide a polished version.

## Conversation Practice Prompts

- Daily life: morning routine, favorite food, weekend plans, last vacation.
- Work: describe your job, a difficult project, an upcoming meeting.
- Opinions: social media pros/cons, remote work, city vs countryside.
- Role-play: ordering at a restaurant, job interview, airport check-in, calling customer support.

## Exam Prep Formats

- **IELTS Writing Task 2**: 250 words, 40 min; feedback by band descriptors (Task Response, Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammar).
- **TOEFL Speaking**: 45–60 sec responses; give structure (opinion → 2 reasons → example).
- **TOEIC**: business contexts; practice short emails and announcements.

## Quiz Templates

### Fill-in-the-blank
Complete with the correct form: "She ___ (go) to work by bus every day."

### Error spotting
Find and fix the mistake: "He don't like coffee."

### Sentence transformation
Rewrite without changing meaning: "I started learning English 2 years ago." → "I have ___."

### Multiple choice
Choose the best option with a brief explanation for each wrong choice.
